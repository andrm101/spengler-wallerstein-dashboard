## Understanding the Work So Far

### The Three Theoretical Frameworks (Integrated)

**Spengler's Morphology**: 10 dimensions operationalized
- Culture vs. Civilization phase transitions
- Lifecycle stages (Birth → Youth → Maturity → Decline → Ossification)
- Soul-form / prime symbol (novel: LDA topic modeling + DNN art analysis)
- Religious form evolution (Mythic → Dogmatic → Reformative → Secular)
- Political form progression (Tribal → City-State → Feudal → Nation-State → Empire → Caesarism)
- Economic form & financialization (sectoral composition + financial depth)
- Urbanization level (% population in cities >50k)
- Artistic-intellectual creativity (novel: GNN-learned composite)
- Mass society / democratization (franchise expansion + literacy + protest)
- External power position (share of global military + economic power)

**Wallerstein's World-Systems**: 10 dimensions operationalized
- Zone classification (Core / Semi-Periphery / Periphery, derived from trade asymmetry)
- Terms of trade (export price / import price ratio)
- Capital intensity ratio (capital stock / labor force)
- Surplus extraction rate (value transferred as % GDP)
- Monopoly rents & quasi-monopolies (patent concentration + commodity dominance)
- Interstate system cohesion (alliance density + conflict frequency)
- Hegemonic cycle position (hegemon share of global power; rising/peak/declining)
- Commodity chain position (raw extraction → semi-manufacturing → high-tech; novel: I-O reconstruction)
- Network centrality in trade (eigenvector, betweenness, closeness from bilateral trade)
- Semi-peripheral stabilization (buffering capacity measured via composite index)

**Cybernetic Binding**: Feedback loops connecting both frameworks
- Positive loops (amplifying): Financialization → Cultural decay; Elite overproduction → Political instability; Hegemonic decline → Crisis
- Negative loops (damping): Semi-peripheral buffering; Trade rebalancing; Cultural renaissance post-decline
- Entropy as measurable state (composite of phase age + financialization + creativity decline + inequality + religious pluralization)
- Bifurcation detection via critical slowing down (autocorrelation, variance acceleration, skewness)
