## Search Quality Rules

**For Scouts to Follow:**

1. **Prioritize peer-reviewed sources**: Journal articles > Conference proceedings > Working papers > Books (books often lack rigor)
2. **Track publication year**: Recent (2010+) > Historical (1980–2010) > Foundational (pre-1980)
3. **Distinguish evidence types**:
   - Empirical: Contains data, statistical tests, quantitative results
   - Theoretical: Conceptual framework, no empirical test
   - Mixed: Some empirical, some theoretical
4. **Flag uncertainty**: If paper has small sample size, limited time period, or admits limitations, note it
5. **Track citations**: Use Google Scholar citation counts to identify influential papers vs. niche work

## Red Flags (Avoid These)

- ❌ Blog posts, opinion articles (not peer-reviewed)
- ❌ Books without DOI/academic publisher
- ❌ Papers with zero citations (may be flawed or niche)
- ❌ Self-published preprints without academic affiliation
- ❌ Data without documentation of collection method
- ❌ Claims without statistical significance tests

**Green Flags (Good sources)**:
- ✓ Peer-reviewed journal articles
- ✓ University press books
- ✓ Working papers from research institutions (NBER, CEPR, World Bank)
- ✓ Government statistical releases (World Bank, Eurostat, OECD)
- ✓ Datasets with published codebooks & methodology papers
